The problem

The verifier walks in. Where are your records?

Every higher-risk NZ food venue runs a Food Control Plan and gets verified by a council or MPI verifier. Fridge and freezer temps, opening and closing checklists, and a written corrective action every single time a temperature is out of range. Today most shops do it on paper — and scramble the week before a verification. Miss one corrective action and you fail. FoodOps makes that impossible.

How it works

Three ways your staff log, zero training needed.

Snap a photo

Staff photograph the fridge thermometer or a delivery docket. FoodOps reads the number, confirms the unit, and files it into your Food Control Plan with a timestamp. Wet hands, one thumb, done.

The moment that sells it

It won't let anyone skip a problem.

A temperature out of range is the #1 thing verifiers fail venues on. In FoodOps, an out-of-range reading turns red and cannot be saved until a corrective action is chosen — moved stock, adjusted thermostat, discarded stock, or called a technician. The record a verifier looks for gets written automatically, at the moment it matters.
